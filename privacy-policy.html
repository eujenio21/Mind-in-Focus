Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Mind in Focus collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our website.

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mind in Focus. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly and transparently.

By accessing or using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you complete a form on our website.

This information may include:

  • Your name;
  • Your email address;
  • Your consent to receive the ebook and related content;
  • Information included in messages that you send to us.

We may also automatically receive limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, referral source, and approximate access time.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected to:

  • Deliver the free ebook that you requested;
  • Send educational content related to focus, productivity, memory, mental clarity, and well-being;
  • Respond to questions or support requests;
  • Improve our website, content, and user experience;
  • Measure website and advertising performance;
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, or security threats;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Email Communications

When you submit your information and provide consent, we may send you the requested ebook and related educational or promotional communications.

You may unsubscribe at any time by using the unsubscribe instructions included in our emails or by contacting us directly.

5. Form Processing

Our website may use FormSubmit or a similar third-party form-processing service to receive information submitted through our forms.

When you submit a form, your information may be processed by that service to deliver the form submission to our designated email address. Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies to understand website traffic, remember preferences, measure advertising performance, and improve our services.

These technologies may collect information such as your browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited, interactions, and referral source.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functions.

7. Analytics and Advertising

We may use third-party analytics or advertising services, including Google Analytics and Google Ads, to understand visitor behavior and measure campaign performance.

These services may use cookies or similar identifiers to collect technical and interaction information. Their use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the website, process forms, deliver emails, analyze traffic, host content, or measure advertising performance.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with a legal obligation, enforce our policies, protect our rights, or prevent fraud or security threats.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, provide requested content, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

You may request deletion of your information by contacting us.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of your information;
  • Withdraw consent for future communications;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Request information about how your data is used or shared.

To exercise a privacy right, contact us using the information provided below. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

12. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

13. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, products, or services that we do not own or control.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

14. International Visitors

If you access this website from outside the country where our services or providers operate, your information may be transferred to and processed in another country.

By using the website and submitting information, you understand that data-protection laws may differ between countries.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, technologies, legal requirements, or business practices.

The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
